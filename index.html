Motor Insurance Claim Frequency: GLM vs XGBoost

Compared a Poisson GLM with XGBoost on 680k French motor policies, with exposure handled as an offset. XGBoost captured about 2.5x the GLM's deviance improvement, and the GLM under-predicted the riskiest decile by around 46%. Deployed as a FastAPI service with Docker and a CI/CD pipeline.
